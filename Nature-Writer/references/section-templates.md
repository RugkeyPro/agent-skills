# Nature 论文各章节段落模板

> 定位：**整段填空骨架**。当你要从零写出一整段、需要一个可直接填空的结构时来这里。
> 单句检索见 `phrase-bank.md`；章节写法与 CEI 概念见 `sections.md`；各刊结构见 `journals.md`。

基于真实顶刊论文语料提炼，可直接参照填写或修改。包含 Nature 正刊、子刊以及 Science 系列的结构模板。

---

## 标题与摘要速查

### Nature 正刊标题风格
- **短而有力**：10–15 个词，突出核心发现
- 例：`Global pesticide budget reveals [X]% reaches the ocean`
- 例：`A global analysis of [X] reveals [unexpected pattern]`
- 例：`[Substance] exposure drives [ecological response] worldwide`

### Nature Communications 标题风格
- **较长但完整**：14–20 个词，包含方法/尺度信息
- 例：`Global mass of buoyant marine plastics dominated by [size class] from [source]`
- 例：`Higher [resolution] mapping reveals [X%] more [feature] than previously estimated`

### Nature 正刊 Abstract 结构
- **四句式**（150–180 词）：
  1. 背景（1–2 句）— 全球重要性或已知事实
  2. 缺口（1 句）— 关键未知
  3. 方法和核心发现（2–3 句）— "Here we..." + 定量结果
  4. 意义（1 句）— 对领域的启示

### Nature Communications Abstract 结构
- **五句式**（180–250 词）：
  1. 背景 + 具体数据支撑
  2. 缺口或方法挑战
  3. "Here we..." + 方法简述
  4. 核心定量结果（2–3 句，含具体数字）
  5. 意义 + 应用前景

## Abstract 模板

### 模板 A（环境污染/全球循环类）
> [Substance X] are ubiquitous environmental pollutants negatively affecting [ecosystem/human health]. About [scale] of [X] are [produced/used/emitted] annually. How much of [X] remains in [environment] and reaches [ocean/groundwater] is uncertain. Monitoring [X] is challenging, and a detailed picture of [mobility/fate] in time and space is largely missing. Here, we develop a [process-based/empirical/statistical] model accounting for [relevant processes] of the [N] most [used/common] [substances/species] to assess [their fate/distribution/impact]. Of the [total input/emission], [X%] is [degraded/stored/transported], [Y%] remains as [residue], and [Z%] reaches [destination]. [Key finding with numbers and units]. Our findings provide [baseline data/new insight] for [risk assessment/management/policy].

### 模板 B（物种分布/生境适宜性类）
> As [an important/a threatened/a commercially valuable] species, understanding the [habitat/distribution] of [species] is highly significant. This study [used/applied] [survey data/occurrence records] from [region] during [period], employing [model] to [map/predict] the [habitat distribution/suitable areas]. Model performance was evaluated using [AUC/TSS/r²], with values [above/exceeding] [threshold], indicating [good predictive ability]. [Key environmental variable] had the highest [contribution/importance] to [distribution/habitat suitability]. Our findings provide [empirical evidence/reference] for [conservation/management] of [species] and for [protected area designation/sustainable fisheries].

### 模板 C（海洋/气候变化影响类）
> [Marine ecosystem / zooplankton / fish communities] play a critical role in [food webs/biogeochemical cycles/fisheries]. Yet, there is limited understanding of how [climate change/ocean warming] will [modify/affect] [community structure/distribution/biomass]. Using a [trait-based/process-based/ensemble] [model/approach] resolving [key components], we find that future [oceans/environments], particularly in [regions], favour [observed shift]. By [mechanism], the [change] partially offsets [negative effect]. However, future [communities/ecosystems] experience [reduced/altered] [function/capacity] as [driver]. These results suggest that [management implication/ecological consequence].

### 模板 D（Nature Communications 五句式）
> [Topic] plays a critical role in [system], with an estimated [X] [units] of [substance/impact] annually. Yet, [key unknown] remains poorly constrained due to [challenge]. Here, we combine [dataset A] and [model/approach B] to quantify [target] across [spatial scale]. We find that [core quantitative finding 1], and that [finding 2 with uncertainty range]. Our results provide a baseline for [application/policy/management] and highlight [region/hotspot] as a priority for [intervention].

## Nature 正刊 "Main" 结构模板

Nature 正刊无独立的 Introduction/Results/Discussion 标题，"Main" 是文章主体，按逻辑自然推进。

### Main 开篇（相当于 Introduction）
> [P1: 建立领域重要性] [Topic] is [globally widespread / a major driver of / central to] [process/ecosystem]. [Quantitative context: X% of / X tonnes per year / X million people]. [Specific example or well-established fact].
>
> [P2: 已有认知与缺口] Previous work has established that [known fact]. However, [key gap – what is not understood]. In particular, [specific unresolved question]. The extent to which [X] affects [Y] at [relevant scale] remains unclear.
>
> [P3: 本研究] Here, we [use/develop/analyse] [approach] to [objective]. [Brief description of approach/dataset]. We show that [core finding in one sentence].

### Main 主体（相当于 Results，无标题）
> [P4: 第一个核心发现] To [objective], we first examined [aspect]. We found that [key result 1 with quantitative detail] (Fig. X). This pattern was [consistent/robust] across [conditions/scales].
>
> [P5: 第二个核心发现] We next asked whether [question]. Our analysis revealed that [key result 2] (Fig. X). [Additional supporting detail]. Notably, [surprising or important nuance].
>
> [P6: 机制/因果分析] What drives this [pattern]? To address this, we [analysed/tested] [variable/mechanism]. We found that [mechanistic finding] (Fig. X). This is consistent with [mechanism/hypothesis].

### Main 尾部（相当于 Discussion，无独立标题）
> [P7: 核心发现总结] Together, our findings demonstrate that [main takeaway]. [Re-state key quantitative finding].
>
> [P8: 与前人对比] Our [estimate/result] is [consistent with / in contrast to] previous estimates, which reported [previous result]. This discrepancy likely arises from [reason].
>
> [P9: 局限性与展望] Several limitations should be noted. First, [limitation 1]. Second, [limitation 2]. Despite these caveats, our analysis provides [main contribution].
>
> [P10: 更广泛意义/结论] More broadly, our results suggest that [general implication]. [Policy/management/outlook implication].

## Nature Communications "Results and Discussion" 合并结构模板

### 首段：背景快速切入 + 本研究目标（1 段）
> [Topic] is a [growing concern / critical component] of [system]. [Quantitative context]. Despite previous efforts, [gap]. In this study, we [approach] to [objective].

### 结果段：发现 1 + 发现 2 + 发现 3
> **[Subheading A]**
> To characterize [X], we [method]. We found that [result] (Fig. Xa). [Detail with numbers]. This was consistent across [conditions] ([statistical test], p = X).
>
> **[Subheading B]**
> We next examined [Y]. Our analysis showed [result 2]. Specifically, [quantified pattern] (Fig. Xb). [Driver/variable] explained [X%] of the observed variance.
>
> **[Subheading C]**
> To understand the mechanisms underlying [pattern], we [analysed/tested] [mechanism]. The results indicate that [mechanistic finding] (Fig. Xc). This may be because [explanation].

### 讨论段：综合 + 对比 + 局限
> **Discussion**
> Our findings provide [new insight] into [X]. The observed [pattern] is consistent with [previous work] but extends it by [new aspect]. However, [discrepancy with some studies].
>
> Limitations of this study include [limitations]. Future work should [direction].
>
> These results have implications for [broader field / management]. [Concluding statement].

## Introduction 段落模板

### P1：全球重要性开篇
> [Topic/system] plays an extraordinary intermediary role in [terrestrial/marine/global] ecosystems. [They/It] integrate[s] [all environmental processes/natural and anthropogenic inputs] within [drainage basins/coastal zones/ocean basins] and export[s] cumulative impacts to [endpoints]. Among [human/anthropogenic] processes, [agriculture/fisheries/urbanization] has the most extensive [spatial footprint/impact], [using/affecting] [scale of area/resources]. [Relevant inputs] are well-recognized sources of [pollution/resource depletion/ecological change], yet [target topic] [has received much less attention/remains poorly quantified].

### P2：背景与已有认知
> [Topic X] are [widely used/abundant/well-documented] for [purpose/function]. [Key data on global scale: production, use, distribution]. [They/It] can [key process], [interact with/affect] [other components], and [potential harm/benefit]. [Environmental fate/mechanism] is complex. [First process]. [Second process]. [Third process]. The [observed concentrations/levels/rates] generally range from [A] to [B] and are associated with [consequence].

### P3：研究缺口（关键！）
> However, [key question/quantity/understanding] has received much less attention [compared to X]. This is possibly because [reason for neglect]. In practice, [X] are more difficult to measure [than Y]. [Challenge 1]. [Challenge 2]. [The few studies/models/estimates] available today [focus only on X/lack critical information/vary by X orders of magnitude]. Conversely, [what is still missing] — needed to [assess/manage/understand] — remains unexplored.

### P4：研究区域/物种/系统
> [Our study system] is [description]. It [inhabits/occupies/covers] [area/region], where [environmental conditions/human pressures/ecological roles]. [Previous studies have shown/it is known that] [relevant background on study system]. [However,] current research mainly focuses on [narrow scope], with a lack of [what this study addresses]. Understanding [X] is crucial for [management/conservation/sustainability].

### P5：研究目标（核心句）
> Here, we [use/develop/present] [method/approach/model] to [objective]. The [dataset/model] is calibrated against [independent observations/field measurements/literature]. We assess [specific aspect 1], explore [specific aspect 2], and quantify [specific aspect 3] across [spatial/temporal scale]. [Our goal/aim] is to [broader purpose, e.g., provide baseline data for management / fill the knowledge gap].

### Introduction 替代结构：两段式（适用于 NCC/NS/NEE 等短 Introduction）

当期刊要求 Introduction 较短（如 NCC 的～2 段）时，标准五段式需压缩为两段：

> **[P1 — 大背景 + 缺口]** [Topic] plays a critical role in [system], with [quantitative scale]. While [known aspect] is well documented, how [gap aspect] remains poorly understood. This knowledge gap severely hampers our ability to [prediction/management].
>
> **[P2 — 具体背景 + 本研究]** [Additional context specific to study system]. Here, we [approach] to [objective]. We find that [core result 1] and [core result 2], revealing [implication].

## CEI 通用段落模板（Claim–Evidence–Interpretation）

适用于 Results 和 Discussion 中任何需要论证的段落。每段遵循：主张 → 证据 → 解释。

### CEI 标准结构
> **[Claim — 段首一句]** Our analysis reveals that [X] drives [Y].
>
> **[Evidence — 中间 2–3 句，含具体数字和统计]** Specifically, [X] accounts for [X%] of variance in [Y], compared to [Z%] for [other factor] (ANOVA, F = X, p < 0.001, Fig. X). This pattern was robust across [regions/scenarios], with [consistent direction] in [X%] of cases.
>
> **[Interpretation — 尾句]** This finding is consistent with [mechanism/hypothesis], whereby [X] influences [Y] through [pathway]. These results suggest that [broader implication].

### CEI 变体 A：因果推断
> Our results establish that [A] causes [B] (Claim). When [A] was [increased/reduced] by [X%], [B] responded with a [Y%] change ([statistical test], p < 0.01) (Evidence). This causal relationship is best explained by [mechanism], a process well-documented in [context] (Interpretation).

### CEI 变体 B：对比论证
> Contrary to previous assumptions, [pattern] is not driven by [conventional explanation] (Claim). Instead, [alternative factor] explains [X%] of the observed variation vs. only [Y%] for [conventional factor] (Evidence). This suggests that [revised understanding] is needed (Interpretation).

### CEI 变体 C：空间/时间模式
> The distribution of [X] exhibits a distinct [spatial/temporal] pattern (Claim). Concentrations are [X]-fold higher in [region/season] compared to [reference], with [min]–[max] range across [sites/times] (Fig. X) (Evidence). This pattern likely reflects [underlying environmental driver], which [mechanism explanation] (Interpretation).

## Results 段落模板

### 模型性能段
> The [AUC/r²/TSS] values for [both/all] [seasons/scenarios/species] were [above/exceeding] [threshold]. This indicated that the selected [environmental variables/predictors] played significant roles in [habitat selection/distribution prediction] for [species]. The [model/simulation] results demonstrated high [discriminatory ability/predictive power], suggesting that the model was adequate for studying [research question].

### 主要发现段（空间分布）
> Regarding [spatial pattern of X], the [distribution/concentration/extent] was [widest/highest/most extensive] in [season/region/scenario], [after which/followed by] [trend description]. However, the [most suitable habitat/highest concentration area] was roughly the same [across seasons/in all scenarios], being primarily [located in / associated with / found near] [specific geographic features/conditions].

### 主要发现段（贡献率/比例）
> The [percentage contributions/relative importance] of [factors/variables] indicated that [dominant variable] had the highest [contribution/importance], with values [above/exceeding] [X%]. Following [dominant variable], [second variable] was a [second/next] dominant [predictor/factor] for [metric]. [Species/scenario-specific differences and what they indicate].

### 时间动态段
> Our [model/analysis] showed strong seasonal variability in [X]. [Quantity] peaked in [month/season] with an estimated [value + unit], and [a low/minimum] in [month/season] with [value + unit]. These seasonal findings were mainly driven by [mechanism, e.g., monsoon, spawning migration, phytoplankton bloom].

## Discussion 段落模板

### 开篇（核心发现提升 — 3 种角度）

#### 角度 A：支持/挑战普遍认知
> Our findings [support/challenge/extend] the general expectation that [broad statement]. [First key finding in general terms], while [second aspect is more nuanced]. Our results [support/provide evidence for] [specific mechanism or hypothesis] — [finding implies mechanism].

#### 角度 B：量化贡献（适用于模型/估算类）
> Our analysis provides, for the first time, a [quantitative/global] estimate of [X]. We find that [X] accounts for [X%] of [total], equivalent to [meaningful comparison]. This [magnitude/scale] highlights the [underestimated/critical] role of [X] in [system].

#### 角度 C：揭示意外发现（适用于正刊/NG）
> Contrary to expectations, our results reveal [unexpected finding]. This [pattern] was not anticipated because [reason], yet it was [consistent/robust] across [conditions]. We suggest that [new explanation] may account for this observation.

### 与前人研究对比（3 种语气）

#### 语气 A：一致
> Our [estimate/finding] is consistent with [ref], who [found/reported/predicted] [similar result] in [system]. The [X] value we obtained ([value]) is comparable to their estimate of [value], despite differences in [method/region/scale].

#### 语气 B：部分一致 + 新发现
> Our results are broadly consistent with [previous study], which reported [previous result]. However, we extend these findings by demonstrating that [new aspect], which was not resolved by [previous approach].

#### 语气 C：存在差异
> Our estimate differs from [previous study] by approximately [X]-fold. This discrepancy likely arises from [methodological difference / data resolution / assumption]. To test this, we [sensitivity analysis], which confirmed that [our result is robust / the discrepancy is explained by X].

### 机制解释（进阶版）
> This [pattern/relationship] may be explained by [mechanism]. [Evidence for mechanism: cite specific data or literature]. [Previous study] also reported [corroborating finding], supporting this interpretation. However, [complicating factor or alternative explanation] cannot be ruled out. We suggest that [refined understanding], whereby [synthesis of multiple mechanisms].

### 局限性（3 种结构）

#### 结构 A：标准三段式
> Several limitations apply to this [work/study/assessment]. First, [main limitation and its effect on results]. Second, [another limitation]. Despite these limitations, [what the study still achieves]. Future [improvements/studies] should consider [specific direction].

#### 结构 B：保守估计强调
> Our estimates are likely conservative because [reason]. For example, [specific source of underestimation]. Therefore, the true [value/impact] may be even [larger/smaller] than reported here. This [conservatism] reinforces our main conclusion that [core finding].

#### 结构 C：稳健性声明
> We acknowledge that [limitation] may introduce uncertainty in our absolute [estimates/predictions]. However, our [relative comparisons / sensitivity analysis / cross-validation] suggests that the main conclusions are robust to this limitation. Specifically, [supporting evidence].

### 子刊专用讨论段

#### NCC：政策和适应意义
> Under projected climate change, the [observed/projected] shift in [X] could have cascading effects on [ecosystem services] and [human well-being]. Adaptation strategies should prioritize [action] in [region], where [vulnerability] is highest.

#### NS：可持续转型路径
> Our results point to [specific intervention] as a critical leverage point for achieving [sustainability target]. This approach, combined with [complementary measure], could reduce [negative outcome] by up to [X%] while maintaining [benefit]. Policymakers should consider [recommendation].

## Conclusions 模板

### 模板 A：标准型（适用于大多数子刊）
> We [projected/estimated/demonstrated] [key finding] for [study system] for the first time. [Quantitative summary of main result]. Among [factors investigated], [dominant variable] had a [significant/critical] role in [shaping/determining] [outcome]. Our [modeling/analysis] [predicts/reveals] [specific implication with numbers]. The findings provided in this study — including [finding 1], [finding 2], and [finding 3] — can be used as [basis/baseline] for [application]. To [address/mitigate] [challenge], [recommended action] should be prioritized. The use of [broader scope/more data/improved methods] will help to [refine/improve/advance] [future work].

### 模板 B：简洁型（适用于 Nature 正刊 1–2 句收尾）
> In summary, we show that [core finding]. These results highlight the [pivotal/underestimated] role of [X] in [system] and suggest that [implication for field/management].

### 模板 C：展望型（适用于 NCC/NS 政策导向）
> Our findings demonstrate that [core finding]. Without [intervention/policy change], [X] is projected to [negative outcome] by [year]. However, [specific action] could [mitigate/reduce] this impact by up to [X%]. These results provide an actionable basis for [policy/management] decisions aimed at [sustainability goal].
