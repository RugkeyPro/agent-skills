# Plot Selection

- Spatial intensity or geography: use `maps`.
- Temporal trend, scenario, event, or cumulative fate: use `time_series`.
- Group differences or uncertainty distributions: use `distributions`.
- Model prediction, ordination, or observed-vs-predicted relation: use `scatter_model`.
- Composition, percent share, contribution, or ranking: use `bar_composition`.
- Feature importance or SHAP effects: use `ml_explainability`.
- Workflow, mechanism, or study design: use `framework`.
