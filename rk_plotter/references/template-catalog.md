## Selection notes
- Prefer `best_for` and `avoid_when` from `scripts/template_registry.py` when two templates receive similar scores.
- Map templates with optional `cartopy` should be skipped or replaced when the environment lacks Cartopy.
- KDE, density scatter, and some interpolation templates may need `scipy`; use histogram or plain scatter fallbacks when SciPy is unavailable.
